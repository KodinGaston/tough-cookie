# 🍪 Tough Cookie Website 🍪

Welcome to the **Tough Cookie** website, a delightful place where you can indulge in a healthy and scrumptious menu. From fresh ingredients to creative dishes, we serve food that not only tastes great but makes you feel good too! 🌱

## 📝 Project Overview

This is a simple, modern, and responsive website that features:

- **Home**: A fresh introduction to the **Tough Cookie** experience, showcasing our vibrant and healthy food options. 🌿
- **Menu**: A sneak peek at some of the amazing dishes that we offer, including new, vegan, and popular items. 🍽️
- **Contact**: A convenient form for reaching out to us, plus important contact info. 💌
- **Link of the proyect**: ✨ https://kodingaston.github.io/tough-cookie ✨

## ✨ Key Features ✨

- **Sidebar Navigation**: A clean, user-friendly sidebar that gives you easy access to Home, Menu, and Contact sections. 📂
- **Grid Layout**: A responsive, elegant grid layout that adapts to various screen sizes for a smooth experience on any device. 📱💻
- **Contact Form**: Reach us directly through the contact form, designed for ease of use and efficient communication. 📬
- **Custom Fonts**: We use beautiful fonts (`Jockey One` & `Roboto Condensed`) from Google Fonts to make the text both elegant and easy to read. 🖋️

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: The backbone of the website structure. 📄
- **CSS3**: A combination of modern design techniques for a sleek, responsive layout. 🎨
- **Google Fonts**: Custom fonts that elevate the design. 💖
- **Responsive Design**: The site looks and works perfectly on any device. 📱💻
